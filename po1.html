Hello, Im Mbakoverua

I’m a passionate youth advocate and emerging technologist committed to creating spaces where young people feel seen, supported, and empowered. Over the past year, I’ve hosted intersectionality-focused events at the LGBTQ Center, volunteered with the Foster Youth Museum to share stories of resilience, and collaborated with Helping Young People Elevate (HYPE) to spotlight youth experiences through art and community engagement. As I continue to grow in my advocacy work, I’m expanding my skill set in technology—learning HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and IT fundamentals—to explore how digital tools can drive social impact. My goal is to bridge youth advocacy and technology, using innovation to amplify voices, improve access to resources, and build inclusive platforms that help young people thrive. I’m passionate about merging creativity, tech, and activism to make systems more equitable and communities more connected.
